2. Collect examples
Send 40 sample messages through the same task. The examples cover billing, bugs, account access, and general questions.
In live mode this makes 40 provider calls and uses your credits. Mubit chooses which examples to keep; 40 calls do not guarantee 25 kept examples or successful training.


3. Follow the learning in Mubit
  1. Open Mubit console → Requests to inspect the real calls.
  2. Open Tasks and look for the support-sorting instructions. Check the kept-example count. Training starts at 25 kept examples by default, when enabled and eligible.
  3. Follow reviewing, training, and testing. In Adapters → History, inspect the result.
  4. For a passing version, open Compare with your provider. Try new messages. These comparisons are not stored and do not train adapters.
This app calls the provider through Mubit. Training status and adapter comparisons are viewed in the console. A passing score measures agreement with the provider, so check the answers yourself too.
